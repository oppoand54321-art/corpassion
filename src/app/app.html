What type of data can Corpassion research?

We focus on publicly available or lawfully obtained business information, company research, market information and professional business data.

Can you build a custom dataset?

Yes. Custom research projects can be structured around your target market, location, industry and required fields.

Can datasets be prepared for CRM systems?

Data can be structured into agreed fields and formats suitable for downstream CRM or spreadsheet workflows.

How do I request a project?

Use the Request Dataset form and provide your target, requirements and desired output.
